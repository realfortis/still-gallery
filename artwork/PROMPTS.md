# Sample artwork prompts

The three JPEGs in this directory were generated using the built-in ImageGen tool and converted to JPEG for the demo site.

## Salt Memory

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: lead artwork for a fictional contemporary gallery website. Create a single wide edge-to-edge abstract oil painting called Salt Memory. The image is the painting itself, straight-on with no frame, wall, text or signature. An almost-black midnight blue sea fills the lower portion. Bold windswept strokes of luminous chalk white and fog blue crash across a diagonal rock shelf, with one small deep burnt red accent and flecks of pale seafoam. Atmospheric outport coastal weather. Rich scraped impasto, grain, visible brushwork, restrained contemporary fine art, striking detail and strong composition.

## Between Headlands

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: sample artwork titled Between Headlands for a contemporary gallery catalog. Make a full-bleed square image of a sophisticated abstract acrylic landscape on rough canvas. Graphic layered fields of dark rust, ochre, charcoal and smoky rose trace a distant headland in low evening light. Visible wash and grain, crisp organic borders. No frame, no lettering, no signature.

## Vessel for Rain

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: sample artwork titled Vessel for Rain for a contemporary gallery catalog. Square fine art studio photograph of a handbuilt sculptural dark graphite stoneware vessel with a rounded base and two uneven flared openings, lit softly from the side against a muted sage-grey plaster wall. Emphasize surface pitting and subtle asymmetry, spare composition, no text.
